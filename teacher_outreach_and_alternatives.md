# No teacher yet: what to do before November 23, 2026, and what else exists

## What I found about who can submit (Solve for Tomorrow, US)

- Students need an adult partner. A source summarising past rules says that is **a teacher employed by your school who is 21 or older**. Only schools that are at least 50% publicly funded qualified in older cycles ([Samsung US eligibility page](https://www.samsung.com/us/solvefortomorrow/), [FAQ](https://www.samsung.com/us/solvefortomorrow/faq/)).
- The 2024-25 rules excluded private schools. The 2025-26 draft is ambiguous (it lists home school and private school among educator categories without clearly saying they qualify) ([2025-26 draft](https://image-us.samsung.com/SamsungUS/home/asset-folders-2025/Samsung-SFT-2025-2026-Rules-Draft-V-1.3_PUBLISH.pdf)). **I could not read the 2026-27 rules, so check them.**
- One entry per educator and email address. An educator can be replaced if they leave.
- So: **a student cannot submit alone.** Without a teacher this cycle, the SFT route is closed.

## If you want to try anyway: a message you can send today

I have not sent anything. Edit the brackets and send it yourself, to a teacher you already know (science, engineering, robotics, CS or a club advisor), ideally in person or by school email.

> Subject: Quick favor: Solve for Tomorrow entry due Monday (Nov 23)
>
> Hi [Teacher name],
>
> I'm working on a project to help reuse old laptop batteries more safely: a low-cost, open-source checker that tests whether a 1–2 hour measurement can predict which used cells leak charge over 28 days. Samsung's Solve for Tomorrow application is due **November 23**, and a teacher has to submit it for the class.
>
> Would you be willing to be the adult partner? I've already written a draft of the pitch and a research plan. I'd do the work. What I need from you is to register the class and submit the application, and to tell me whether our school allows supervised battery testing. If the school says no to cells, I have a fallback plan that uses new cells or a partner lab.
>
> Samsung also runs free virtual clinics for educators. Could we talk for 10 minutes [today / tomorrow]?
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name], [grade]

**Tips**
- Ask a teacher who already supervises hands-on work. Check with the school before promising battery work.
- Don't ask a teacher to commit to supervising battery testing in the same breath. The application and the later hands-on work are separate asks, and the second can be decided after Nov 23.
- Have the draft pitch ready to hand over: [sft_application_pitch.md](sft_application_pitch.md).
- If it's not possible in three days, do not rush a weak entry. The project is not tied to this deadline.
